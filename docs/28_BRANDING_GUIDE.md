# SANAD — Branding Guide

## Name
SANAD

The name should communicate trust, documentation, support and professional business operations.

## Personality
- trustworthy
- precise
- calm
- minimal
- premium without luxury decoration
- professional in Arabic and English

## Typography
Choose a font family or compatible pair with:
- excellent Arabic legibility
- excellent Latin legibility
- clear numerals
- good small-size table readability
- modern but not trendy/fragile

Avoid overly geometric Arabic fonts that harm long-form readability.

## Logo direction
Wordmark-first is preferred initially.

SANAD should render well:
- English: SANAD
- Arabic: سند

Do not overcomplicate the logo.

## UI color philosophy
Neutral professional foundation.
Use accent color sparingly.
Statuses must not rely on color alone.

## Documents
Mostly monochrome/neutral.
Minimal accent if needed.
Information clarity comes first.

## Tone
Clear business language.
Avoid playful copy in operational screens.
